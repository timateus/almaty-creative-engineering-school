Example courses

Examples of what we build for a specific museum or group of artists.
01
3D Scan: Glitch & Memory

Workshop

For museums

A workshop on scanning sculpture and on what goes wrong in the process. Participants scan works from an exhibition, keep the glitches, holes and stitched pieces, and build a new piece from the fragments. We talk about archiving and memory, and about what a record keeps and what it loses.

Result: a 3D archive of the exhibition, and the participants' assemblies.

02
Museum Surfaces

Workshop

For museums

A workshop on the surfaces of a museum building. Participants sample walls, floors and objects with a scanner, microscope, endoscope and clay casts, then assemble a new piece from the samples. Wear, cracks and repairs become an archive of the building, and the errors of each instrument stay in it.

Result: a texture library of the building, and the participants' assemblies.

03
Making Friends with Plants

Field workshop

For museums, schools

A field workshop on getting to know the plants around us. Participants walk the grounds, identify the plants with field guides and apps, and make their own set of plant cards. A field guide is also an instrument with its own assumptions, and we look at them.

Result: a map and species list of the grounds.

04
AI Tools for Self-Archiving

Workshop

For artists

A workshop for artists on putting a practice in order. Participants use AI tools to describe, tag and connect their works and documentation into a personal archive, and check what the AI gets wrong or makes up along the way.

Result: a personal archive and a routine to keep it.

05
AI Tools for Portfolio

Workshop

For artists

A workshop for artists on building a portfolio with AI tools: texts about works, a statement, a CV, a site or a PDF. We look at what AI does to the language of an artist's text and how to keep your own voice.

Result: a finished portfolio.
